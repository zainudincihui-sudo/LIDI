About LIDI

LIDI is a social intelligence platform for on-chain traders. Our job is simple: show you who moves first, so you can catch momentum before it shows up everywhere else.

The problem

By the time a token is trending on a chart or all over social media, the early movers have already taken their position. The real edge isn't the price chart — it's knowing which wallets acted before the crowd did.

What LIDI does

Trending Tokens surfaces memecoins gaining volume and holders in real time. Smart Wallets ranks addresses by track record, so you can see who's consistently early and profitable. Live Activity streams on-chain transactions as they happen. Leaderboard ranks the best-performing traders over time. Follow & Alerts lets you track specific wallets and get notified the moment they act.

How we're building it

LIDI is being built in stages, from the ground up:

V1 — Discovery: tokens, wallets, and live activity.
V2 — Social: follow, profiles, and leaderboards.
V3 — Intelligence: the LIDI Score and smart alerts.
V4 — AI: automated analysis that surfaces opportunities for you.
V5 — Copy Trading: follow a trader's strategy directly.

Built on Robinhood Chain

LIDI reads directly from Robinhood Chain, indexing wallet activity and token movement so the data you see reflects what's actually happening on-chain — not a delayed or curated feed.

LIDI is under active development. Product details and figures shown across the site will evolve as new features roll out.

Last updated: 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how LIDI handles information when you use the site.

2. Wallet connections

If you connect a wallet, we use only your public address to personalize your view — for example, to save which wallets you follow and to show relevant alerts. LIDI never asks for, transmits, or stores your private keys or seed phrase, under any circumstance.

We may collect basic usage data (such as pages visited or features used) to understand how LIDI is used and to improve it. We do not sell personal data to third parties.
